How to play
1. Answer a full-screen question before rolling.
2. Correct = roll the dice. Wrong = next player's turn.
3. Ladders move you forward; snakes send you backwards.
4. Reach exactly space 100 to win. Questions are shuffled every new game.
Science fact: Fertilization happens when a sperm cell fuses with an egg cell. This game is a simplified classroom model.
